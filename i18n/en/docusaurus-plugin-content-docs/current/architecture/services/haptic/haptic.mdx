# Haptic Service

The haptic service maps system feedback requests to the platform vibration device. When the device is missing or an operation is unsupported, the service reports according to device capabilities and operation results; a silent no-effect does not constitute a success confirmation.

The service provides three global strength levels — off, normal, and enhanced — as well as feedback semantics such as short, stronger, and long. The specific waveform and duration are determined by the platform implementation; callers express feedback intent and do not directly control the vibration hardware.

Core synchronously invokes the Vibrator's on/off operations according to feedback intent; the Vendor determines the actual waveform and device strength. The operations have no return value, and the current Service does not provide per-vibration completion or hardware success events, so applications cannot rely on a given request necessarily producing a perceptible vibration. See [Platform/Vendor Contract](../../platform_contract) for the platform boundary.
